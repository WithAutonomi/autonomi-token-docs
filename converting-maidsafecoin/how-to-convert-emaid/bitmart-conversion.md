# BitMart Conversion

If you have your eMAID on the [Bitmart](https://www.bitmart.com/) exchange, they will complete the conversion for you automatically.

This process will take between 3-5 working days after Autonomi tokens has been listed, which is currently anticipated to be on the **11th of February, 2025**, but is still subject to change.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Withdrawals, deposits and trading will be suspended at 11:00 GMT on February 6th, 2025**

If you wish to complete the conversion process yourself, or don't want Bitmart to convert your to Autonomi tokens for you, you should withdraw your eMAID from the exchange prior to this date.
{% endhint %}
